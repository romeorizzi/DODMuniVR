# Topics
   - paths, trees, and forests in undirected graphs
   - the connected components of a graph
   - Yes and No certificates (NP and co-NP)
   - characterization of connected graphs
   - good conjectures and characterizations
   - characterization of bipartite graphs (either a spanning tree as a Yes-certificate or a 2-coloring that singles out one component)

# Summary
   - partitions and equivalence classes are equivalent concepts
   - the reachability relation in undirected graphs is an equivalence relation
   - the connected components of a graph, the BFS visit of a graph
   - Yes and No certificates (NP e co-NP)
   - characterization of connected graphs (Yes-certificate: a spanning tree. No-certificate: a 2-coloring that singles out one component)
   - good conjectures and characterizations
   - definition of bipartite graphs as 2-colorable graphs (i.e., in terms of a Yes-certificate, in NP)
   - characterization of bipartite graphs (odd cycles as NO-certificates)
