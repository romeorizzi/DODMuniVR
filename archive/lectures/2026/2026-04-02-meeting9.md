# Summary
   - using an ILP solver to search for counterexamples to a good conjecture:
     to obtain a graph where every node cover is larger than every matching, we guess the number of nodes n, a minimum node cover C and a maximum matching M with |C|=|M|+1 among polynomially many possibilities in n (using automorphisms and forced relations between M and C. In fact |M|=1 will work. We ban out every bigger matching with an ILP with a singly-exponential number of constraints (or through a structural understanding), and (then) every smaller node cover with an ILP with a singly-exponential number of constraints (or through a structural understanding/obvious monotonicity)
